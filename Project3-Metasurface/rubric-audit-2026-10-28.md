## Numerical claims that must stay together

At missing-training widths 0/0.0135/0.027, blocked median joint right-field errors are 9.7902/14.2814/20.2759% for physics training and 0.3543/0.5787/1.6389% for direct-reference training. Nested training counts are 256/248/240. Each pair shares its initial weights, model, inputs, optimizer and 1,200 gradient calls. The full-coverage objective contrast is 27.63x; the largest-gap direct-reference error rises 4.63x. This removes support rather than relocating a fixed number of samples.

All six blocked deployment gates fail. Three supervised off-grid gates pass. The full-coverage supervised arm recovers 10/10 feasible designs but wrongly promotes 10/150 infeasible designs, with R95=0.04135 above 0.03. Better median fields alone do not establish useful screening.

The separate optimizer control uses a covered checkpoint and a different physical two-segment voltage metric: Adam 43.39% versus L-BFGS 7.57%, with 204/256 blocked state errors improved. Both retain 0/8 feasible designs and fail their validation gate. These metrics must not be compared as though they were the same cohort/field norm as the gap study.
